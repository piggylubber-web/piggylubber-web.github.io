Zombie Takeover Support

SlideTracked: Zombie Takeover · Carter Clan Studios

Playing

Tap a picture tile beside the empty square to slide it. Finish puzzles to earn their artwork in the Art Gallery. Your unfinished boards are saved on this device.

Swap Tokens

Each Swap Token unlocks unlimited swaps for one puzzle attempt. Closing the app keeps that access. Completing or reshuffling ends it. Every additional board or replay needs its own token. Buying tokens adds them to your balance; choose a puzzle when you are ready to activate one.

Purchases

An active yearly pass provides picture preview, any-order access to released volumes and ad-free play. A separately purchased volume keeps sequential play and ads. Use Restore Purchases in Pass & volumes for eligible Apple purchases. Sign in to the same player account to retrieve an unused token balance.

Manage subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Deleting your player account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Contact

Email piggylubber@icloud.com with your app version, device model and a description of the problem. For a TestFlight issue, include the build number. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
