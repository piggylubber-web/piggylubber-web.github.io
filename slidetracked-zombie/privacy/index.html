SlideTracked: Zombie Takeover
Privacy Policy

Updated September 11, 2026 · Carter Clan Studios

This policy covers Zombie Takeover, including its TestFlight builds. TestFlight purchases are sandbox transactions and current test builds use Google test ad units.

Saved puzzles

Puzzle positions, completed artwork, settings and activated swaps are stored on your device. They are not uploaded as a cloud gameplay save. Removing the app may remove this progress, subject to your device backup settings.

Optional player account

You can play as a guest. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple and Appwrite provide authentication. Appwrite stores a player identifier, the name or email Apple supplies (including a private relay address if chosen), sessions and technical connection information such as IP address and device information. We use your player identifier to protect your token balance and connect purchases to your player account.

Purchases and Swap Tokens

Apple processes payments; we do not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat processes an app-specific identifier, purchase history, subscription status and technical information to verify purchases, restore eligible access and operate purchase analytics. Guest players may receive an anonymous app identifier. Our Appwrite wallet records token credits and activations so a repeated transaction does not add or spend tokens twice. Puzzle artwork and tile positions are not sent to RevenueCat.

Advertising

Players without an active yearly pass may see Google AdMob banners and full-screen ads. Google may process IP address, device and app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. In builds offering adult opt-in, players who select 18 or older may allow tracking through Apple so advertising partners can use the device advertising identifier to personalize ads and measure advertising across apps and websites, subject to regional consent choices. If you decline, we request non-personalized ads and you can keep playing. Older builds request only non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing. You can change Apple tracking permission in device Settings.

For players who select under 18 or do not disclose an age group, we use restrictive ad and consent settings, request non-personalized ads, and do not request Apple tracking permission. The age-group choice is kept for the current app session. Use Ad settings when a different person plays. Google consent choices are collected where required, and ad privacy options are available when Google indicates they are required. Yearly subscribers do not receive ads.

Account deletion and choices

Use Delete account in Player account to request removal. You will sign in with Apple again to confirm your identity. Deletion removes the player account and unused token balance, revokes the Apple sign-in token and requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer profile. Local puzzle progress and earned artwork remain on the device. Separate transaction-operation records are retained to prevent duplicate credits and maintain transaction integrity. Contact us about personal information in retained records or a deletion that did not finish.

Deleting the game account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its policies. Other service providers may retain records under their own obligations and policies.

Families

The game includes zombie and horror-themed artwork. Follow its App Store age rating and supervise younger players. Parents can use Apple purchase restrictions and Ask to Buy. Do not enter a child's personal information in support messages. A parent or guardian can contact us about an account or personal information supplied by a child.

Security and service providers

Account and purchase-service communications use HTTPS. Apple, Appwrite, RevenueCat and Google may process information in countries other than your own. We use access controls for player accounts and the wallet. No service can guarantee absolute security.
