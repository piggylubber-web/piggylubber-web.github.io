SlideTracked Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

SlideTracked is a sliding-picture puzzle game published by Carter Clan Studios. We designed it to work without accounts, social profiles, chat, location access, camera access, microphone access, contact access, or third-party advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores puzzle progress, settings, solved-game records, move counts, and consumable Swap Token balances locally on the device. This information supports gameplay and is not used for behavioral advertising.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat to retrieve localized product information, complete purchases, restore eligible purchases, and determine whether a Master Pass or Volume entitlement is active. RevenueCat may process an app-specific user identifier, purchase history, subscription status, product identifiers, device or app technical information, and diagnostic events needed to operate purchases. Carter Clan Studios does not receive full payment-card details.

Advertising and studio promotions

SlideTracked does not integrate a third-party advertising network and does not use targeted or behavioral advertising. The app may occasionally show a static, first-party Carter Clan Studios artwork spotlight. That spotlight does not track the player, open an advertiser profile, or transmit personal information.

Sharing

If a player deliberately uses the device share button, the operating system opens its standard share sheet. The player chooses whether to share and which service receives the message. Carter Clan Studios does not receive the destination or the shared message through that feature.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly request or collect a child's name, email address, precise location, contacts, photographs, voice recordings, or messages. The app contains no chat, public posting, personalized advertising, or third-party ad tracking. Purchase controls are provided by the App Store and should be managed by a parent or guardian through the device's parental controls and Ask to Buy settings.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child supplied personal information to Carter Clan Studios, please contact us using the method below. We will investigate and delete information under our control when required.

Data retention and choices

Local gameplay information remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed, subject to device backup behavior. App Store purchase records are retained by Apple under Apple's policies. RevenueCat retains purchase-service records according to its contractual and legal obligations. Users may manage subscriptions in their Apple ID subscription settings and may use Restore Purchases inside SlideTracked.

Security and international processing

Purchase-service communications use encrypted HTTPS connections. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase information in countries other than the player's country, subject to their privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Parents and guardians can send privacy questions or deletion requests to piggylubber@icloud.com. Visit our SlideTracked support page for purchase and gameplay help. Do not send a child's personal information through a public issue tracker.
